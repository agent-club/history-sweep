A few questions. Other ways to install.

Can I undo a deletion?

No. Deleting a URL removes all of its visits, including those outside your date range. Review your selection first. Chrome may sync deletions across signed-in devices.

What permissions does it need?

History supports search and deletion; favicon displays website icons. ActiveTab and scripting support user-invoked website entries and the in-page modal. ContextMenus adds right-click access; storage saves local preferences. No access to passwords or cookies.

Does it search every record?

Each search reads up to 100,000 distinct URLs by last visit. A notice appears at the limit; older records may be outside the results.

Prefer a manual install?

Download the v0.4.0 local package

  1. Unzip the downloaded ZIP.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Choose “Load unpacked” and select the extracted folder.

The Chrome Web Store and local package may serve different versions.

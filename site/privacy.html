What the extension does

History Sweep has a single purpose: helping you search, select, and delete Chrome browsing history in batches.

What it processes

When you search, the extension reads page URLs, titles, last-visit times, and visit counts through Chrome’s history API. Search results stay in the extension window’s memory. Confirmed deletion uses a temporary in-memory background queue that continues when the window closes. Completed queue results are discarded within 60 seconds, or earlier when the worker stops. Tasks do not resume after Chrome exits or the extension reloads.

What is saved locally

The extension saves only your language preference in persistent local storage, not a copy of your history. Search conditions pass to the full page through a one-time memory handoff and are not included in its URL. This website also stores only your language preference.

Transfers and limited use

The extension sends no browsing history and contains no analytics, advertising, or remote scripts. We do not collect, sell, or share browsing records. Information accessed through Google APIs is used solely for these visible features, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use restrictions.

Permissions and deletion

The only requested permission is history. The extension does not access passwords, cookies, bookmarks, or page content. Deletion is initiated and confirmed by you, removes all visits to each selected URL, and cannot be undone. Chrome’s own synchronization settings may propagate deletions to other signed-in devices.

This website

This site adds no analytics, advertising, or third-party fonts, and product images use illustrative data. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver, secure, and operate the site; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Contact and release status

The website is publicly available at sweep.agentclub.dev. The Chrome extension remains a pre-release and is not yet listed on the Chrome Web Store. For support or privacy questions, contact us through GitHub Issues.

What the extension does

History Sweep has a single purpose: helping you search, select, and delete Chrome browsing history in batches.

What it processes

When you type a keyword for search suggestions or start a search, date filter, or current-website lookup, the extension reads page URLs, titles, last-visit times, and visit counts through Chrome’s history API. Search results and suggestion indexes stay in the extension page and its local Worker memory. Confirmed deletion uses a temporary in-memory background queue that continues when the window closes. Completed queue results are discarded within 60 seconds, or earlier when the worker stops. Tasks do not resume after Chrome exits or the extension reloads.

What is saved locally

The extension saves your language preference in persistent local storage and your user-entered kept-domain rules and opening preference in chrome.storage.local on this device only, not a copy of your history. Search conditions and date filters pass to the full page through a one-time memory handoff and are not included in its URL. This website stores only your language preference.

Transfers and limited use

The extension sends no browsing history and contains no analytics, advertising, or remote scripts. We do not collect, sell, or share browsing records. Information accessed through Google APIs is used solely for these visible features, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use restrictions.

Permissions and deletion

The history permission supports searching and deletion, activeTab supports user-invoked current website searches or the in-page modal, scripting inserts the chosen modal host, contextMenus adds the website right-click entry, and storage saves local kept-domain rules and the opening preference. The extension does not access passwords, cookies, bookmarks, or page content. Website entries only start searches, without automatic selection or deletion. Date filters match a URL's last visit; confirmed deletion removes all visits to each selected URL, including visits outside the date range, and cannot be undone. Chrome’s own synchronization settings may propagate deletions to other signed-in devices.

This website

This site adds no analytics, advertising, or third-party fonts, and product images use illustrative data. The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver, secure, and operate the site; see the Cloudflare Privacy Policy for details.

Contact and release status

The website is publicly available at sweep.agentclub.dev. The Chrome extension is publicly listed on the Chrome Web Store. For support or privacy questions, contact us through GitHub Issues.
